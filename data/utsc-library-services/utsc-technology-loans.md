# Technology loans

The UTSC Library offers a Technology Lending Collection for coursework, study, and creative projects.

## Categories and Pickup Locations

Technology loans are divided into three pickup and booking categories:
1. Self-service course reserves area: Smaller items (cables, adapters, calculators, headphones).
2. Info Desk: Large electronic devices requested at the desk (laptops, monitors, cameras, projectors).
3. MS Bookings (through Makerspace): Specialized production equipment booked in advance.

## Return Policy and Locations

- Large electronic devices (laptops, monitors, cameras, etc.) must be returned directly to the Info Desk.
- Small items should be returned to the wooden "Book return" boxes located inside and outside the library entrance.
- Makerspace items must be picked up and returned during Makerspace operating hours.
- Late returns: Late returns are subject to a fee of $0.50 per item per hour.

## Available Equipment and Loan Terms

### Computing & Displays
- Windows Laptop: Windows 10 laptop equipped with MS Office 365, MS Teams, Zoom. Comes with 1 laptop and 1 power adapter. Requested at Info Desk.
- Portable Monitor (15.6 inch): Supports laptops, PCs, and Apple/Mac operating systems with Mini-HDMI 1.4 and USB-C inputs.

### Calculators
- Graphing Calculator (TI-84 Plus): All-purpose graphing calculator. Mini USB cable and QuickStart guide included.
- Scientific Calculator (TI-30Xa): Battery-operated scientific calculator with math and science functionality.
- Financial Calculator (BA II Plus): Financial calculator with business and finance calculation capabilities. Loan period: 3 hours. 3 available.

### Audio & Video Equipment
- Kodak Luma 150 Portable Wireless Projector: Pico pocket projector with HDMI and Micro USB cables. 60 ANSI lumens, 16:9 widescreen, battery up to 2.5 hours. Loan period: 3 hours.
- Logitech QuickCam Pro 9000 Webcam: 2.0 MP, USB 2.0. Loan period: 3 hours.
- Blu-ray Burner: BUFFALO Portable USB 3.2 Blu-ray Drive. Loan period: 72 hours.
- Blu-Ray Players: Nutromo players (5-hour loan) and Sony SDP-S3100 (72-hour loan).
- Headphones: Over-ear headphones connecting to standard 3.5mm headphone jacks. Loan period: 3 hours. 8 available.
- Wireless Microphones: For smartphone/tablet recording via Makerspace. Loan period: 3 days.

### Power, Cables & Accessories
- Universal Laptop Power Adapter (Powseed 70W): Universal charger with multiple tips for various laptop models.
- External Battery Charger (Poweradd Slim2): 5000mAh portable charger with USB 5V/2.1A output.
- HDMI Cables (6-foot): Connects laptops to monitors/projectors.
- Ethernet Cables (6-foot): High-speed network cable. Loan period: 3 hours.
- USB 3.0 Card Reader: Multi-format memory card reader. Loan period: 3 days.
- Cricut Mini Heat Press & Lighting Kits: Available through Makerspace booking.
