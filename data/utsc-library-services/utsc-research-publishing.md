# Research and publishing services for faculty

The UTSC Library partners with faculty and academic researchers at every stage of the research cycle, from funding proposals and data management to dissemination, open publishing, and impact evaluation.

## Research Help and Consultations

- In-Depth Consultations: Dedicated consultations with subject liaison librarians to develop search strategies, identify primary literature, and navigate collections.
- Digital Scholarship Librarians: Consult on data manipulation, digital tools, visualization, computational text analysis, and digital methodology.
- Entrepreneurship & Innovation Librarians: Guide market research, patent searches, and commercialization pathways.

## Publishing & Repository Platforms

- TSpace Research Repository: Free, institutional open-access repository to preserve and disseminate scholarly papers, preprints, reports, and working papers produced by faculty and graduate researchers.
- Journal Production Services: Free hosting and technical infrastructure for faculty-led and student open-access academic journals published at U of T.
- Pressbooks: Online document and open-textbook publishing platform for creating accessible open educational resources (part of eCampusOntario's Open Library).
- U of T Dataverse in Borealis: Bilingual, secure Canadian research data repository supporting open discovery, management, preservation, and DOI minting for research datasets.

## Faculty Services and Delivery

- InfoExpress: Premium document delivery service available exclusively to U of T Scarborough faculty, staff, and graduate students. InfoExpress retrieves books and articles from any U of T campus or partner institution globally and delivers them directly to office desks or digital inboxes.
- Storage Stacks Browsing (Highland Hall): Faculty members and graduate researchers may request appointments to browse archival storage stacks in Highland Hall.
- Web Project Archiving: Liaison librarians preserve at-risk faculty research project websites and digital scholarly outputs in the campus web archives.

## Grant-Funded Research and Data Support

- Research Data Management (RDM): Support for developing Data Management Plans (DMPs), data deposit, and funder compliance (Tri-Agency requirements).
- In-Kind Grant Support: Library services and expertise can be formally written into grant proposals as institutional in-kind contributions.
- Classroom & Lab Collaboration: Liaison librarians teach research methodologies directly in academic courses and collaborate on multi-year grant projects employing student researchers.
