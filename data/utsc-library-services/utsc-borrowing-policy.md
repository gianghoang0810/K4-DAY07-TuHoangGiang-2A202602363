# UTSC Library borrowing policy

You will need a TCard or library card to borrow from the libraries. If you lose your TCard, you must report it to the library to ensure material is not charged to your account. TCards are issued by the TCard office. You can add funds to your TCard using TCard+.

## Loan Privileges by Patron Type

Loan privileges at most University of Toronto Libraries:

| Patron Type | Loan Periods | Maximum Potential Loan Periods | Item Limits |
|-------------|--------------|--------------------------------|-------------|
| Undergraduate students | 14 days | Unlimited | 50 |
| Graduate students | 90 days | Unlimited | 200 |
| Faculty members | 90 days | Unlimited | 200 |
| Staff | 90 days | Unlimited | 200 |

### Other Borrowers

| Patron Type | Loan Periods | Maximum Potential Loan Periods | Item Limits |
|-------------|--------------|--------------------------------|-------------|
| Alumni | 14 days | 14 days (no renewals) | 25 |
| Research readers | 14 days | 42 days | 100 |
| Direct borrowers | 14 days | 42 days | 100 |
| Associate members | 14 days | 42 days | 100 |
| Continuing studies students | 14 days | Unlimited | 50 |

## Exceptions

- Items from the Popular Collection, periodicals, audio visual material and other Special Collections (e.g. Academic Skills, Cookbooks).
- Loan periods for items from short term loan collections, including course reserves and the Technology Lending Collection, vary between 3, 5, 24 or 72 hours and cannot be renewed.

### Popular Collection
The popular collection includes books, DVDs, magazines, video games, and board games. Located in the Reading Room near the front entrance of the library.

| Item | Loan Periods | Maximum Potential Loan Periods | Holds |
|------|--------------|--------------------------------|-------|
| Books | 14 days | 1 renewal, 14 days | Yes |
| DVDs | 14 days | 1 renewal, 14 days | Yes |
| Magazines | 14 days | 1 renewal, 14 days | Digitization only |
| Board Games & Video Games | 14 days | 1 renewal, 14 days | Yes, UTSC pickup only |

### Periodicals
- Loan period: 4 days
- Renewals: No renewals
- Item limits: 5 items

## Designate a Proxy Borrower
- Faculty and staff may designate another library borrower to sign out items onto their account.
- This service is also available to any library borrower unable to visit the libraries because of a disability.
- Complete the proxy application form to request this service (UTORid login required).

## Renewals & Returns

### Unlimited Renewals
Current U of T students (including Continuing Education), faculty, and staff get unlimited renewals for regular loans as of February 2022.
Loans will only become due:
- If another user makes a request for a checked out item.
- If there is a block on a user's account.
- If the user's borrowing privileges expire.
Users will be notified by email when an item must be returned.
*Not eligible for unlimited renewals: Course reserves and other short term loans; technology items; audio/video items; Popular and special collections; interlibrary loans.

### Automatic Renewals
Two days before an item is due, the system will automatically try to renew it on your behalf.
If not eligible for renewal, you will get an email asking you to return it. If another borrower requests the item, your loan period shortens to 14 days if currently longer.

### Returns
- Materials can be returned at the Information and Reference Desk, or drop box across from the desk when unstaffed.
- Regular circulating items can be returned at any U of T library.
- Drop box outside the library is available when the library is closed.

## Request an Item & Holds
- Sign in to LibrarySearch, find the item, select "REQUEST: Library Pick Up", choose location, and send request.
- Holds can be requested on items that are in the library as well as checked out items.
- When ready, notified by email; held for 7 days.
- Limits: Faculty, staff, and graduate students may place up to 25 concurrent requests. Other users can place up to 10 concurrent hold requests.
- Short term loan items (course reserves and technology collection) cannot have holds placed on them.

## Recalls
- Any loaned item may be subject to recall if another borrower places a hold.
- Users receive email notification and must return recalled items within 14 days.
- Overdue recall fine: $2.00 per day until returned. Overdue recalls block borrowing privileges.

## Fines and Penalties
- Privileges withheld when an item is more than 14 days overdue or fines/fees reach $25.00 or greater.
- Privileges withheld when short term loan items (including course reserves) are more than 24 hours overdue.

Fines schedule:
- Regular books or media: $0.50 per day per item
- Course reserves & technology lending materials: $0.50 per hour per item
- Recalled items: $2.00 per day per item

Payment methods:
- Online: Sign in to LibrarySearch and pay fines.
- In person: Information and Reference Desk by Credit Card (VISA, MasterCard, AMEX) or Debit.

Fine forgiveness reasons that do NOT qualify: forgot due date, did not receive courtesy notice, unaware of library policies, borrowed for someone else, returned late by someone else, did not report TCard stolen.

## Fees for Lost & Damaged Items
Items not returned within 42 days of the due date are deemed lost.

Standard replacement fees:
- Lost book, CD, DVD, VHS, video or board game: $145.00 per item
- Lost bound journal: $145.00 per item
- Lost unbound journal: $75.00 per item
- Lost charging cable, battery pack, headphones: $25.00 per item
- Lost pico projector, camcorder, microphone: $300.00 per item
- Lost laptop charger: $50.00 per item
- Damaged item repair fee: $45.00 per item

Patrons may purchase a replacement copy of a lost book or board game and pay a $45.00 processing fee instead of the standard replacement cost (subject to staff approval).
