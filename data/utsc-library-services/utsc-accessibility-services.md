# Services for persons with disabilities

The UTSC Library strives to ensure that the library and its resources are fully supported and accessible. Students who have a disability are encouraged to register with AccessAbility Services.

## Extended Library Services and Facilities

### Physical Access and Entrances
- Elevator located near the entrance of the library.
- Automatic door openers are provided at:
  - Library entrance
  - Washrooms
  - Group study rooms: AC251A, AC252, AC255, AC284, AC285
  - Silent study room: AC338
  - Mindfulness space: AC258
  - Archives: AC260
  - One Button Studio: AC286

### Retrieval of Books
Library staff assist patrons with disabilities in retrieving library materials:
- Request by phone to Information and Reference Desk (416-287-7500) or in person.
- Information required: Call number, title, UTORid.
- Retrieved items are checked out and held for three days at the desk.

### Specialized Equipment and Furniture
- Closed circuit television magnifier (CCTV) - reservation optional.
- Adjustable chair and height-adjustable table.
- Accessibility software on all Info Commons computers: ZoomText (screen magnification and text-to-speech) and Kurzweil 3000.
- Contact staff for assistance with CCTV or to sign out the adjustable chair.

### Photocopying and Alternative Formats
- Photocopiers and printers are available at the front of the library.
- Enlarged photocopies of course reserves can be made upon request and picked up the following business day.
- Publications in alternative formats: Braille, large print, electronic disc, or audiocassette available upon request via AccessAbility staff.
- Service animals are welcome following the University of Toronto service animal guidelines.

## Responsibilities of Persons with Disabilities

- Registration: Students are encouraged to register with AccessAbility Services to arrange accommodations.
- Making needs known: Library users are responsible for informing staff of their specific needs.
- Reporting whereabouts: For safety reasons, patrons requiring assistance entering and exiting should report arrival, location, and departure to InfoDesk staff. In emergencies, staff must know the location of mobility-impaired individuals for fire department evacuation (elevators do not operate during fires).
- Proxy borrower authorization: Patrons who cannot visit in person may designate a proxy borrower by completing a Pickup Authorization Form at the Information and Reference Desk. The patron remains responsible for all items checked out by their proxy.
