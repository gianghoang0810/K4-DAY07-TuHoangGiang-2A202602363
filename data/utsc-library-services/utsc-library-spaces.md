# Library spaces and services

The UTSC Library provides diverse study environments, specialized technology facilities, and academic resource spaces.

## Study Environments

- Silent Study Space: Dedicated silent study area located in room AC338. Strict quiet policies apply to support focused, distraction-free study.
- Study Rooms: Eight small group study rooms available for collaborative academic work. Rooms must be booked in advance via the campus Intranet.
- Mindfulness Space: Located in room AC258, offering quiet seating and equipment supporting mental well-being.

## Creative and Media Production Spaces

- Makerspace: A safe, welcoming environment equipped with creative technology and fabrication tools to support experimentation and project creation.
- Podcast Room: Located in room AC252A. Equipped with an audio mixer, professional microphones, and monitor headphones for podcasts, voice-overs, and streaming.
- One Button Studio: Located in room AC286. Automated video recording studio allowing easy high-definition video presentations at the push of a button.

## Academic & Community Resources

- Information and Reference Desk (Info Desk): Located at the front entrance of the library. Staffed during regular hours for borrowing, technology checkouts, reference help, and inquiries.
- Archives and Special Collections: Located in room AC260. Houses rare and unique historical materials supporting teaching and research. Reading room access is by appointment.
- Course Reserves and Short Term Loan: Located 20 steps to the left of the Info Desk for physical reserve materials.
- Seed Library: Located across from the main floor washrooms. Free seed checkout for community gardening; patrons take what they need and donate harvested seeds back.
- Reuse Depot: Community sharing station for stationery, notebooks, pens, and school supplies.
- The BRIDGE: Joint partnership between the Library and the Department of Management integrating business research, data terminals, and entrepreneurship.
- Vitrine Exhibits: Physical exhibition spaces at the front of the library created in collaboration with campus community members.
