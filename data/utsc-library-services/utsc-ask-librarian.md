# Ask a Librarian chat service

Get research and library help from anywhere via real-time chat with university librarians and library staff.

## Services Provided

Use Ask a Librarian chat to:
- Find research sources and databases on any academic topic.
- Locate specific books, journal articles, or course materials.
- Navigate library services, citation management, and research tools effectively.

## Operating Hours

Active term hours (January 5th - April 11th, 2026):
- Monday - Thursday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Friday: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Saturday - Sunday: 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM

Holiday Closures:
- Monday, February 16, 2026 (Family Day)
- Friday, April 3, 2026 (Good Friday)

## Privacy and Transcripts

- Ask a Librarian chat collects only the minimum amount of information necessary to provide effective reference service.
- At the conclusion of each chat session, users can print or email a complete transcript of the conversation for their research records.
