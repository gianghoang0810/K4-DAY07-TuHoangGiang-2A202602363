# Course reserves and short-term loans

## What are Course Reserves?

Instructors can make course readings available online through Quercus, the University's online learning management system, or as physical items that can be borrowed from the Course Reserve / Short Term Loan section at the UTSC Library.

## Physical Course Reserves Location

- Check your syllabus or contact your instructor for information about where assigned course readings are located.
- Instructors set aside (reserve) physical items such as books and other course readings in the Course Reserves/Short Term Loan section at the UTSC Library.
- Physical course reserves are located 20 steps to the left of the InfoDesk.

## Online Course Reserves via Quercus

Instructors can make assigned online course materials available through the Library Reading List module on your Quercus course page. Materials include articles, books, web links, and media resources.

How to access online reserves:
1. Look for the Library Reading List app in your Quercus course navigation menu.
2. The courses in which you are enrolled and which have Library Reading Lists will be listed.
3. Select the specific course you want to launch its course reading list.

If you have trouble finding or accessing online course readings in Quercus, consult the Library Reading List guide or contact your liaison librarian.

## Instructor Requests and Inquiries

- For instructors: Course reserves can be requested through the instructor request workflow.
- Questions or feedback: Email readinglist-library@utsc.utoronto.ca.
